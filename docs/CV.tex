\section{Professional Summary}

Computational physicist and scientific machine learning researcher working at the intersection of lattice field theory, mathematical physics, and generative AI. My research develops multi-scale theoretical and computational frameworks based on effective field theory, perturbation theory, and asymptotic analysis. More recently, my work has focused on machine learning methods for lattice field theory.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Research impact:} 26 peer-reviewed publications, h-index 19, 3000+ citations.


% ============================
\section{Flagship Research Contributions}

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Precision lattice QCD and Standard Model parameters:}
Contributed to high-precision determinations of quark masses, meson decay constants, and hadronic matrix elements through the MILC, Fermilab Lattice, HPQCD, and TUMQCD collaborations. These results are incorporated into global reference averages, including FLAG and the PDG.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\item \textbf{Effective field theory for multi-scale QFT:}
Developed and applied effective field theory frameworks (Symanzik EFT, ChPT, HQET) to systematically connect lattice simulations with continuum QCD observables, enabling controlled multi-scale analyses.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\item \textbf{MRS scheme:}
Developed the minimal renormalon-subtracted (MRS) scheme, providing a systematic treatment of renormalon ambiguities in perturbative QCD without introducing an arbitrary subtraction scale, enabling stable multi-scale analyses and high-precision heavy-quark mass determinations.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\item \textbf{Diffusion models for lattice gauge theory:}
Developed an implicit score-matching framework for Lie groups, enabling generative modeling for SU($N$) lattice gauge field configurations.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\item \textbf{Scientific machine learning infrastructure:}
Developed GPU-accelerated software frameworks for diffusion models, normalizing flows, and differentiable scientific computing.

\end{itemize}
