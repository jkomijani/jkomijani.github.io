\section{Professional Summary}

Computational physicist and scientific machine learning researcher working at the intersection of lattice field theory, mathematical physics, and generative AI. My research develops multi-scale theoretical and computational frameworks based on effective field theory, perturbation theory, and asymptotic analysis. Since 2022, my work has focused on generative models for lattice field theory.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Research impact:} 27 peer-reviewed publications, h-index 20, 3426 citations (\href{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EBV_Sl0AAAAJ&hl=en}{Google Scholar}, Oct 2026).

\section{Flagship Research Contributions}

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Precision lattice QCD and Standard Model parameters:}
Contributed to high-precision determinations of quark masses, meson decay constants, and hadronic matrix elements through the MILC, Fermilab Lattice, HPQCD, and TUMQCD collaborations. These results are incorporated into global reference averages, including FLAG and the PDG
[\href{https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.98.054517}{PRD 98, 054517 (2018)};
\href{https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.98.074512}{PRD 98, 074512 (2018)}].

\item \textbf{Effective field theory for multi-scale QFT:}
Developed and applied effective field theory frameworks (Symanzik EFT, ChPT, HQET) to systematically connect lattice simulations with continuum QCD observables, enabling controlled multi-scale analyses.

\item \textbf{MRS scheme:}
Developed the minimal renormalon-subtracted (MRS) scheme, providing a systematic treatment of renormalon ambiguities in perturbative QCD without introducing an arbitrary subtraction scale, enabling stable multi-scale analyses and high-precision heavy-quark mass determinations
[\href{https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.97.034503}{PRD 97, 034503 (2018)}].

\item \textbf{Diffusion models on Lie groups:}
Developed an implicit score-matching framework for Lie groups and predictor--corrector samplers with Hamiltonian-dynamics and Langevin correctors for SU($N$) lattice gauge theories
[\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06134}{arXiv:2605.06134}];
introduced a noise schedule on Lie groups that yields a linear decay of the Wilson action with diffusion time
[\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17326}{arXiv:2605.17326}];
co-developed group-equivariant diffusion models for lattice field theory
[\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP07(2026)213}{JHEP 07, 213 (2026)}].

\item \textbf{Normalizing flows for lattice field theory:}
Identified the multi-scale structure near critical points as a source of poor scaling of generative models and proposed architectures inspired by effective field theory
[\href{https://doi.org/10.22323/1.430.0019}{PoS Lattice 2022, 019}];
introduced SVD-based gauge-equivariant flows for SU($N$) together with a parametrization of SU(3) eigenvalues
[\href{https://doi.org/10.22323/1.466.0050}{PoS Lattice 2024, 050}];
reformulated two-dimensional SU($N$) gauge theory as independent plaquettes learned by small spline flows, combined with an exact reweighting
[\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09147}{arXiv:2610.09147}].

\item \textbf{Scientific machine learning infrastructure:}
Developed GPU-accelerated software frameworks for diffusion models, normalizing flows, and differentiable scientific computing (lattice\_ml \& normflow).

\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Nonlinear eigenvalue problems:}
Introduced analytical and numerical methods for nonlinear eigenvalue problems arising in nonlinear systems with separatrix structure
[\href{https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/47/23/235204}{J.~Phys.~A 47, 235204 (2014)};
\href{https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/ac2e29}{J.~Phys.~A 54, 465202 (2021)}].

\item \textbf{Hyperasymptotics:}
Developed and applied hyperasymptotic and resummation techniques for perturbative expansions in QFT, enabling controlled treatment of divergent series and renormalon ambiguities
[\href{https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/47/23/235204}{J.~Phys.~A 47, 235204 (2014)};
