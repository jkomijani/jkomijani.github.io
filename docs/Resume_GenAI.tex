\section{Professional Summary}

Theoretical and computational physicist working on physics simulations, statistical inference, and generative modelling. I develop research ideas end to end, from mathematical formulation and algorithm design to PyTorch implementation and computational experiments. My distinctive expertise, rooted in effective field theories (EFTs), is identifying the collective variables that drive large-scale behaviour in complex systems, and using them to interpret data and to construct efficient quantitative models with controlled uncertainties. Notably, I independently developed the theory, EFT model, and statistical analysis for precision quark masses.

\vspace{0.4em}In machine learning, I combine this perspective with equivariance to develop efficient generative models, and I am interested in how these principles can inform interpretability, distillation (matching an effective model to a more complete description), and the understanding of scaling behaviour.

\vspace{0.5em} \textbf{Research impact:} \href{https://jkomijani.github.io/docs/Publications.pdf}{27 peer-reviewed publications}, h-index 20, 3,500+ citations (\href{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EBV_Sl0AAAAJ&hl=en}{Google Scholar}, Nov 2026).

\section{Selected Research Contributions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Precision lattice QCD and Standard Model parameters}: Combined three effective field theories into a single effective description, used as a statistical model that extrapolates expensive simulations to physical limits with a full error budget. Key contributor to high-precision determinations of quark masses and meson decay constants, with results incorporated into the world reference averages of the Particle Data Group. Introduced a new quark-mass scheme (MRS) that removes the leading divergence of perturbation theory without an arbitrary cutoff, improving the precision of quark-mass determinations. 10+ papers; e.g., \href{https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.98.054517}{Phys.~Rev.~D~98, 054517 (2018)} (quark masses); \href{https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.98.074512}{Phys.~Rev.~D~98, 074512 (2018)} (decay constants).

\item \textbf{Diffusion models on Lie groups}: Developed diffusion models for SU($N$) gauge theories with a Hamiltonian-dynamics corrector; co-developed group-equivariant diffusion models. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06134}{arXiv:2605.06134} (JHEP, accepted); \href{https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP07(2026)213}{JHEP 07, 213 (2026)}.

\item \textbf{Sampling with exact correction} (single author): Factorized 2D SU($N$) gauge theory into independent plaquettes and a single global variable. Sampled the plaquettes with normalizing flows and the global variable with a conditional normalizing flow, followed by an exact reweighting correction. Sampling is unbiased, fully parallel, and free of Markov-chain burn-in; the flows act on local objects, so training is cheap and does not grow with lattice size. Phys.\ Rev.\ D (accepted), \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09147}{arXiv:2610.09147} \textbar{} \href{https://github.com/jkomijani/holonomy-2d-gauge-notebooks}{notebooks}

\end{itemize}
