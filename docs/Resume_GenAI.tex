\section{Selected Research Contributions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Theory, statistics \& generative AI}: Developed a single-author framework for two-dimensional SU($N$) lattice gauge sampling by reformulating plaquette-to-link consistency as a conditional sampling problem. Derived group-commutator densities for SU(2) and SU(3), combined analytical sampling with learned normalizing flows and importance-weight correction, and validated the method against Hybrid Monte Carlo (HMC). Tests cover $2\times2$ and $32\times32$ lattices, with reported per-plaquette acceptance rates above 99\%. \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09147}{arXiv:2610.09147} \textbar{} \href{https://github.com/jkomijani/holonomy-2d-gauge-notebooks} {Reproducible notebooks}


\item \textbf{Independent research in computational physics}:
Independently developed the theoretical framework, effective-field-theory model, and statistical analysis for precision determinations of five quark masses using lattice-QCD data generated by the MILC Collaboration. Addressed perturbative renormalon divergence and developed the minimal-renormalon-subtracted (MRS) mass framework to connect theoretical predictions with simulation results. This work resulted in a trilogy of papers and contributed to precision quark-mass determinations reported in FLAG/PDG reviews.



\item \textbf{Efficient generative models}: Developed symmetry-aware normalizing flows and diffusion models, including Lie-group score matching, gauge-equivariant architectures, and analytically derived noise schedules. Designed an EFT-inspired Fourier-space layer with approximately 3.4K parameters and a 1,288-parameter SVD-based gauge-equivariant flow block that achieved effective sample size comparable to a 288-layer spectral-flow baseline on a $4^4$ SU(3) lattice.

 %\item \textbf{Scientific computing and mathematical physics}: Contributed to precision lattice-QCD determinations of Standard Model parameters reported in FLAG/PDG world averages; developed methods for resummation of divergent perturbative series, quark mass determination, and nonlinear eigenvalue problems.
\end{itemize}
